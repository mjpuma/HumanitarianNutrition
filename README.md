# HumanitarianNutrition (v0.1, research in development)

Historical evidence for future humanitarian nutrition interventions, 1954–2026.

A companion to [FamineWatch Datasets](https://github.com/mjpuma/faminewatch-datasets) and [TherapeuticProvisioning](https://github.com/mjpuma/TherapeuticProvisioning).

The study begins with Public Law 480 in 1954 and traces the evolution of specialized nutrition products, their procurement costs, quantities and supply chains. Historical coverage is a research target, not a claim of complete observations.

**Status:** Unpublished seed database; independent source verification and redistribution review are pending. Not operational or clinical guidance.
